Why naive RAG fails

Legal research fails when structure is flattened.

Finnish tax answers are usually not in one paragraph. They live across statutes, guidance, amendments, definitions, and exceptions.

01 Chunking cuts logic - rules and exceptions split apart.

02 References stay inert - section links are not followed.

03 Time matters - superseded rules can rank too highly.

"What withholding-tax rate applies to a foreign specialist with key-personnel status?"

A correct answer needs a statute, a Vero interpretation, and the amendment that changed the rate and validity window.

Top-k returns proximity. The task needs provenance.
