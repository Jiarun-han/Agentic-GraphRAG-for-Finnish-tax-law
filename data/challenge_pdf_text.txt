N E R
0 1
Entity extraction
Pull domain entities out of
regulation text: articles,
taxes, agencies, deadlines,
monetary thresholds.
Domain-specific NER
beats generic models —
you have the corpus.

0 2
Relation extraction
Type the edges. Amends ·
repeals · transposes ·
references · applies-when.
Mix rule-based parsers
(clear citation forms) with
LLM-as-extractor for the
looser cases.
Section-awareparsing — notchunking
Drop fixed-size windows.
Use the regulationʼs own
units: book → title →
article → paragraph. The
hierarchy carries scope
and inheritance; let
retrieval walk it.

Embed each node's text —
its definition, summary or
surface forms. A query
finds the right entry points
by vector similarity; the
graph carries the walk
from there. Fuzzy entry,
structured traversal.
